# Design system: Overnight

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One night, told by its sky"**

Overnight has one visual world across two surfaces: the menu bar popover (380 by 620) and the documentation site. Both are plain macOS at rest: system type, system grays, system status colours, and on the popover grouped white cards on a light gray ground. The world lives in one place on each surface, a sky that carries the time of night. In the popover that sky is the hero at the top, which turns to full night while the Mac is held awake, to an unlit dusk while armed but letting it sleep, and to night warming into dawn the morning after. In the docs the same sky owns the frame and every picture: the first viewport is a starry night where a drawn MacBook closes its lid as the moon rises, the chapter openers and the sticky running head move from dusk through night and rose to dawn and daylight as the reader moves through the night, and each drawing is a round window onto the sky of the moment it shows.

The moon is the only ornament. The crescent and star from the menu bar icon appear in the popover hero, the docs wordmark and running head, the hero scene and inside the drawings, and its gold marks the docs' step numbers, the dotted path between them, the numbers in how-to lists, the typing dots and the primary button. The docs show the product only as drawings: flat vector scenes in the night palette, lavender metal and an indigo screen glow, never screenshots, code or tables. Everything outside the sky and its windows stays calm: a reading column of 68ch on a near-white ground, with no cards.

Density differs by surface. The popover is a compact settings panel (13px body, 34px rows, 10px outer margins). The docs read like a picture book for adults (17px body, 1.6 line height, large drawings, generous chapter padding).

**Key characteristics:**
- The sky is the only expressive layer; everything else is native macOS.
- Hero, opener and window skies are state and time, never decoration.
- One filled accent per popover screen: the main button.
- Status colour words, never colour alone; small status text uses the text-safe tokens.
- Light and dark themes for both surfaces, following the system setting.
- Docs pictures are flat drawings in the night palette inside round windows; motion plays once and rests on the last frame.

## Colors

A deep indigo night with a gold moon and an amber dawn, framed by Apple's system neutrals and status colours; in the docs drawings, lavender metal under an indigo screen glow.

### Primary
- **Night indigo** (night-a) to **night violet** (night-b): the held-awake sky. The popover's on hero is a 160deg gradient between them. The docs hero sky runs dusk-a 0%, night-a 52%, night-b 100% at 165deg. Night-a is also the ink on gold and amber (docs buttons, step numbers, the dawn opener and bar) and the disc behind each number in a how-to list.
- **Deep night** (night-deep): the bottom of the night opener and the top of the drawings' night sky.
- **Moon gold** (moon): the moon and star, the docs' primary button and bar Download, step numbers, dotted path arcs, numbers in how-to lists, typing dots, the clock in night openers, the skip link and text selection (at 55% alpha). In drawings it is the lit face of things: the padlock, the sand, the sun, the lamp's light. On the popover it appears only inside the hero.
- **Moon ink** (moon-ink): moon gold dark enough for a light ground. The Day clock, list bullets, the footer moon and the moon on the daylight bar. In dark it becomes moon gold.
- **System indigo** (accent): the interactive accent for the popover's bars, progress fills, checkboxes and timeline dots, and the docs' links. It stays the same in both docs themes only through the accent-text pair below.

### Secondary
- **Dusk slate** (dusk-a to dusk-b): the night without its glow. Popover standby hero, the top of the docs hero sky and the running head's default.
- **Rose** (rose, with rose-mid at 60%): the usage-limit hour. The rose opener and rose window run night-b, rose-mid, rose. The running head uses #4a3a68 in this phase.
- **Dawn amber** (dawn): the morning. The popover's morning hero runs night-a 0%, night-b 55%, dawn 140% at 172deg, so only the lower edge warms. The docs' dawn opener (#c48468 to #d49464) and running head turn amber with night-a type.

### Tertiary
- **Accent fill** (accent-fill, accent-fill-dark): the accent behind white text, used only on the primary button. White on it measures 5.65:1 light and 5.06:1 dark.
- **Accent text** (accent-text, accent-text-dark): the accent as text, link, secondary button label and focus ring. In dark it lifts to a pale indigo so it reads on dark cards (6.16:1 on the dark card). The docs' single accent token uses the same two values.
- **Status fills** (green, orange, red and their dark values): Apple's system colours, for switches, dots, bars, tints and the tab badge. Never for small text on light grounds.
- **Status text** (green-text, orange-text, red-text and their dark values): darker light-theme values that pass AA on white and on their own 14 to 18% tints (4.66 to 4.98:1). In dark, green and orange text equal the fills and red lifts to a lighter coral.

### Neutral
- **Popover ground and cards** (popover-bg, popover-card): grouped-list gray behind white cards, Apple's system values. Dark uses #1c1c1e behind #2c2c2e.
- **Popover text, muted, faint**: primary text, secondary values and sublabels, and disabled or idle marks (idle dots, the off-state moon outline).
- **Hairlines**: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08) in the popover, rgba(28, 28, 30, 0.12) in the docs; white at the same alphas in dark.
- **Controls**: rgba(118, 118, 128, 0.12), 0.24 in dark. The track behind tabs, segmented controls, inputs and switches.
- **Docs ground, ink, muted** (docs-ground and family): a calm reading column, slightly cooler than the popover's ground. Dark ground is near-black. Muted carries notes and the descriptions in the watch list.
- **Daylight** (docs-day): the last opener, the install chapter.
- **List disc in dark** (docs-disc-dark): the how-to list disc lifts from night-a to a lighter indigo so it stays visible on the dark ground.

### Drawing palette
The docs drawings use only this palette, the skies and moon gold.
- **Lavender metal** (metal-light, metal, metal-mid, metal-edge, metal-shade, metal-deep, metal-dark, light to dark): the MacBook, lamp, padlock shackle, battery, thermometer, hourglass frame and folders. The lit top of a surface takes the lightest step, faces in shade step down. The closed lid is a gradient from #eef0f8 to metal.
- **Screen and bezel** (screen, bezel): the open lid's display inside its bezel, a phone's body. Bezel also tops the deep night sky and grounds the desk under the lamp.
- **Screen glow and seam** (screen-glow, screen-seam): the indigo light of a working Mac. Screen-glow fills the radial glow of an open screen and the blurred band under a closed lid; screen-seam is the line of light along the closed lid and the bars that stand for typed text. They share their values with the popover's dark accents.
- **Skies**, each a top-to-bottom gradient: dusk (dusk-b to dusk-a), night (night-deep to night-b), deep night (bezel to night-a), rose (night-b, rose-mid at 60%, rose) and dawn (night-b, #9a6a78 at 55%, #e3a070). The app icon tile is night-b to night-a.
- **Drawn UI** borrows the popover's own tokens: popover-bg for the menu bar, panels and notifications, accent-fill for the drawn Keep awake tonight button, popover-text for the menu bar moon and type bars, and red-text-dark coral for a warning shape (low battery, heat).
- **Shade on gold**: #e9bf55 and #c9a640 for the shaded side of a gold object.

### Named rules
**The sky carries the time rule.** Night, dusk, rose, dawn and day colours appear only on surfaces that show the time or state of the night: the popover hero, the docs hero, chapter openers, running head and the drawings' round windows, where the sky is the hour of the moment drawn (dusk to start, night while Claude works, rose at the usage limit, dawn in the morning). Content cards never take them. The one small exception is the night disc behind a how-to number.

**The one filled accent rule.** In the popover the only filled accent is the main button. Selected tabs and segments are raised white segments, not accent fills.

**The text-safe status rule.** Status words and small status text use green-text, orange-text and red-text. The bright fills are for shapes. Tinted surfaces mix the fill into the card (12 to 18%) and keep text in the text token; secondary text on a callout mixes the text colour 78% with the callout's tone instead of gray.

**The two accents rule.** Text and rings use accent-text; white-on-accent uses accent-fill. They are the same in light and differ in dark. Never put white text on accent-text-dark or use accent-fill-dark as link colour.

**The dark duplicate rule.** The popover's dark theme is declared twice in src/styles.css: once under prefers-color-scheme for the app, once on [data-theme="dark"] for the preview's theme=dark. The two blocks must hold identical values; change both in the same edit. The docs have a single dark block.

**The drawn night rule.** A drawing uses only the drawing palette, the skies, moon gold and the popover's own tokens for drawn UI. Drawings look the same in light and dark; only their lift deepens.

## Typography

**Display font:** the system stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, SF Pro Text, Helvetica Neue; the docs add Segoe UI)
**Body font:** the same system stack
**Label/mono font:** ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace, for the popover's ntfy topic field only

**Character:** San Francisco everywhere, because the product is a macOS utility and its docs describe one. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, not from a second face.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (docs-display): the docs hero headline, once, on the night sky.
- **Headline** (docs-chapter, with docs-clock beside it): chapter openers and the three-steps heading. The clock (8 PM, 11 PM, 2 AM, 8 AM, Day) sits right, in moon gold on night grounds, night ink on dawn, and moon ink on daylight.
- **Title** (docs-heading, docs-step-title, docs-subheading, docs-item-title, popover-title): headings inside the docs column at 28px; the three step titles at 22px; sub-sections at 21px; watch-list and question titles at 18px; the popover hero headline.
- **Body** (docs-lede, docs-lead, docs-body, docs-note, popover-body): the hero sentence at 20px (18px under 700px) in 84% white, up to 30em; a chapter's opening paragraph at 19px; paragraphs at 17px in a 68ch column; notes and step asides at 15px, notes in muted. Watch-list descriptions sit at 16px in muted. The popover's base text at 13px.
- **Label** (docs-small, popover-group-label, popover-small, popover-caption): the docs bar, the version line and the footer at 14px; the popover's group headers in uppercase muted gray, hint and note text at 12px, sublabels, timestamps and project names at 11px.
- **Buttons and numbers** (docs-button, docs-step-number): the hero buttons at 18px semibold; step numbers at 19px bold; how-to list numbers at 14px bold.
- **Figures** (popover-figure): hero stats, log stats, times, sizes, the docs clock, the bar's time and the how-to list numbers use tabular figures. The free-space figure is 20px semibold.

### Named rules
**The one face rule.** No web fonts and no display face. Monospace appears only for the popover's ntfy topic.

**The tabular time rule.** Every time, duration, percentage and size uses tabular-nums so it does not jitter as it updates.

**The named control rule.** In the docs, the name of a button, switch or setting is set in 600 weight wherever the text mentions it, in the same words the popover uses.

## Layout

**Popover.** A fixed 380 by 620 panel, one column. Hero, then tabs (Tonight, Storage, Log), then a scrolling main, then a footer with lid status and Quit. Outer margins are 10px; group cards pad 10px by 12px; the hero pads 14px; rows are at least 34px with hairlines between them. Group headers sit 14px above and 6px below their card, inset 4px.

**Docs hero.** Under the 52px bar, a night sky in two columns inside a 1240px frame: text up to 30rem on the left, vertically centred with 56px below it, and the hero drawing filling the right and bleeding to the viewport edge, standing on the sky's bottom edge. Gaps run 24 to 56px and the top padding 32 to 56px with the viewport. Under 900px the text stacks above the drawing, which then spans the full width. The three steps begin directly below the sky.

**Steps.** A 1140px frame with 44px above. Three equal columns, 40px apart, each a 200px round window, then title and text. Dotted arcs join window to window. Under 860px the steps stack 48px apart, windows drop to 168px and the arcs are hidden.

**Chapters.** Each chapter is an opener band (44px top, 40px bottom; 32 and 28 under 700px) aligned to the 68ch column, then the column (40px top, 72px bottom, 24px gutters, 20px under 700px). A drawing beside text sits in a 180px window at the right of the column, 40px from the text, with 56px between consecutive pairs; under 700px the window moves above its text at 140px. The watch list is a two by two grid (32px rows, 36px columns) of 76px windows with text 18px beside; one column under 700px. The install drawing is a full-column pill up to 560px. Anchors scroll with a 64px offset for the running head.

**Running head.** As the reader scrolls, the bar shows the current chapter's time and name and takes on its phase colour: dusk, night, rose, dawn (amber with night ink), day (frosted ground with a hairline). The bar always carries a gold Download button at its right. The phase line hides between 861 and 1100px to make room for the section links; under 860px the links hide and the phase line returns; under 480px the phase line hides and Download moves right. Under 480px the hero buttons stretch to share the row.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat and tonal. Depth comes from the ground and card contrast and from hairlines. Shadows are small and functional in the popover. In the docs one soft lift sets each drawing off the page, and inside the drawings depth is painted: soft ellipse shadows, radial glows and a blurred seam of light, never outlines.

### Shadow vocabulary
- **Raised segment** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`): the selected tab and selected segment.
- **Switch knob** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`): the white knob of a switch.
- **Live dot halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px` green at 20%): a working session's dot.
- **Moon glow** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 0 6px rgba(247, 214, 128, 0.55))`): the filled moon in the held-awake hero only.
- **Drawing lift** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 14px 24px rgba(20, 22, 50, 0.22))`, dark 0.5 black): every round window, side window, watch window and the install drawing. The hero drawing takes none; it is part of the sky.
- **Step number cut** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 5px` the page ground): a ring of ground colour that separates the gold step number from the window it overlaps.
- **Daylight bar hairline** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0` the docs hairline): the running head in the day phase.
- **Painted depth** (inside drawings): a flat ellipse in #0b0d29 at 50% under the MacBook; a radial moon glow from gold at 35% to clear; a radial screen glow from screen-glow at 40% to clear; the seam glow, a screen-glow ellipse blurred 5px.

### Named rules
**The flat card rule.** Cards, groups and callouts carry no shadow; a hairline or a 1px inset line is enough. In the docs only drawings lift, and text never does.

## Shapes

Soft, Apple-like rounding on a small set of steps: 12px for the popover panel and the docs hero buttons, 10px for the popover hero, groups and callouts, 8px for popover buttons, tab and segment tracks, the hero note, the docs bar Download and skip link, 6px for segments, inputs and pills, 4px for focus rings in the docs, and full circles for dots, step numbers, list discs and the docs' drawing windows. Borders are 1px hairlines. The crescent-and-star mark is the one shared symbol, used by the app icon, tray icons, popover hero, docs wordmark and every drawing that shows the app.

Docs drawings are round windows: a 240 by 240 scene clipped to a circle and shown at 200, 180, 168 or 140px, or a 120 by 120 scene at 76px. The install drawing is the one exception, a pill with fully rounded ends. Inside, everything is flat filled vector shapes with rounded corners; text on drawn screens and panels is rounded bars, except a drawn button label. Strokes appear only where the object is a line (a lamp arm, a padlock shackle, a battery case, a check mark, steam) or where a real macOS element has an edge (the pointer, a sheet's hairline); a filled shape never gets a dark keyline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px in the popover and the docs bar, 12px for the docs hero buttons).

### Motion (docs)
Every drawing's resting state is its last frame, so without JavaScript or under prefers-reduced-motion the page is complete and still; all motion lives inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference and only runs once the page marks itself as scripted. Drawings start paused and each plays once, the first time 30% of it is on screen; the hero plays on load. Arrivals use an exponential ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)); the lid folds on a steep ease-in (cubic-bezier(0.55, 0, 0.8, 0.2), 0.55s) and the back of the lid lands on the ease-out (0.5s). In the hero the moon rises 80px over 2.6s, the typed lines grow in 0.15s apart, the caret blinks twice, the glow fades and the lid folds at 1.4s, the bubble pops at 2.5s and the dots begin at 3s. The three steps play 0.6s apart when in a row and the dotted arcs draw in left to right; the watch items play 0.25s apart. Each later drawing performs one action: the app drops into Applications and the moon pops into the menu bar with a gold ring, the button is pressed and the password sheet rises, the lamp goes out and the lid closes, the padlock opens and shuts, the battery drains from green to coral, the thermometer rises, the alert slides in and the phone buzzes, the hourglass empties and a play button pops, the moon sets and the sun rises, the app slides into its folder. Two loops carry the signature: the typing dots (1.3s, 0.15s apart, in the hero and step 3) and the seam glow breathing between 35 and 90% (3.2s, hero only). The build also loops the hero's stars (a 3.6s twinkle) and the steam over the morning cup (2.6s).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the night, dusk, rose and dawn colours on surfaces that tell the time or state of the night, including the drawings' windows, matched to the hour drawn.
- **Do** use green-text, orange-text and red-text for any status text, and the bright fills only for shapes and tints.
- **Do** use accent-fill behind white text and accent-text for links, labels and the focus ring.
- **Do** keep the two dark blocks in src/styles.css identical; change both in one edit.
- **Do** keep one filled accent per popover screen, the main button.
- **Do** use tabular figures for times, durations, sizes and percentages.
- **Do** draw docs pictures as flat vector scenes in the drawing palette, inside a round window with the drawing lift.
- **Do** make every drawing complete on its last frame, play it once when it scrolls into view, and keep all motion inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference.
- **Do** keep a visible focus ring on every control, switching to white or moon gold on night grounds.
- **Do** turn off transitions under prefers-reduced-motion and on the popover's first draw.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second typeface or a web font; monospace is for the ntfy topic only.
- **Don't** put the night gradient behind content cards, callouts or tables.
- **Don't** fill a selected tab or segment with the accent; raise it on the card colour.
- **Don't** use the system orange, green or red fills as small text on light grounds.
- **Don't** show invented users, numbers or press.
- **Don't** put screenshots, code blocks or tables in the docs; technical material lives in README.md.
- **Don't** add shadows to cards and groups; use a hairline.
